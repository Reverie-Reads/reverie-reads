# ISBNdb source-use review

Historical September 8 status: **live subscription-value acquisition held**. This is a practical source-fit assessment,
not legal clearance. Public pages were read in a browser on September 8; no account settings changed.

The [terms](https://isbndb.com/terms-and-conditions), dated September 7, permit subscriber applications
and limited operational storage during an active subscription, including a trial. They restrict
systematic extraction/analysis into derivative collections and require deletion of API-derived data
within 30 days after expiry, including incorporated data. Independently obtained data and bare ISBNs
are distinguished. These conditions leave unresolved whether our systematic evaluation and durable
shared-catalog enrichment fit. An ordinary subscription is not demonstrated permission for graph
building, model sharing, or permanent post-cancellation retention.

The [pricing page](https://isbndb.com/isbn-database) currently lists Basic at $14.99/month, 5,000 daily
searches, one call per second, and a seven-day trial. These are public plan limits, not a readout of
this account's remaining quota. The [API specification](https://api2.isbndb.com/doc.json) confirms
that bulk ISBN requests still consume quota per ISBN. Capacity is not the immediate blocker.

Finish offline safeguards, but do not run the new study or promote ISBNdb-derived catalog data until
the source-use question is resolved. Seek written clarification only if the owner chooses to do so;
otherwise favor sources compatible with durable independent metadata. Do not change billing, erase
historical reports, or infer that existing aggregates are covered/exempt without review.

The new CLI refuses live execution before input/env access, with no override. No new provider calls,
real frame freeze, subscription cancellation, legal contact, production write or data deletion was
performed in this review. Synthetic tests cannot establish source permission or provider accuracy.
