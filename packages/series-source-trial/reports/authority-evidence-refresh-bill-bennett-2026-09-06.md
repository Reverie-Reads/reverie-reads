# Authority evidence refresh: Bill Bennett

Date: 2026-09-06

## Outcome

*Swimming with Manatees* moves from the candidate queue into authority-reviewed gold as book one
of the Ava Martinez series. This is a human evidence refresh of an already selected work, not a new
sampling frame and not a model-generated truth change.

The verified [Bill Bennett Goodreads Author blog](https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/64464831.Bill_Bennett/blog)
provides the missing relational evidence. Bennett explicitly names the Ava Martinez series,
describes *Swimming with Manatees*, *Borealis*, and *The Lambeau Directive* as the first three Ava
Martinez thrillers, and calls *Swimming with Manatees* Ava's first case. Together those statements
establish exact-work membership and publication position one.

The official Selfies shortlist and eligibility FAQ remain selection provenance for the 2025
independent-publication stratum. They do not become series-truth sources. Retail and community
labels remain unnecessary to the ruling.

## Program impact

The development set remains 263 selected works and advances from 163 to 164 reviewed cases. It now
contains 137 reviewed series-positive cases, 27 true standalone controls, and 99 unresolved
candidates. The recent independent or Kindle-first stratum advances from 48 to 49 reviewed cases,
leaving one case to reach its 50-case development minimum. The overall review gap is 36; the true
standalone gap remains 23 and the publisher or author standalone-challenge gap remains 15.

No provider request, authority-scout call, resolver call, or token spend was needed. The source was
found during targeted human review after the original truth-blind scout had safely abstained.

## Boundary

This refresh changes only trial data, tests, and documentation. It does not write Supabase, alter
the corpus, deploy an Edge Function, or turn a reviewed gold label into an automatic production
decision.
