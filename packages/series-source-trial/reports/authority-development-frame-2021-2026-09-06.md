# Authority development frame: 2021 Kindle Storyteller shortlist

Date: 2026-09-06

## Outcome

The complete five-work 2021 Kindle Storyteller shortlist adds five authority-reviewed positive
series cases to the development partition. No case was selected based on provider behavior or ease
of review.

| Work | Authority result | Series | Position |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| *Escape to the Hummingbird Hotel* — Daisy James | Reviewed series-positive | The Hummingbird Hotel | Unknown |
| *Stranger at the Villa* — Mary Kingswood | Reviewed series-positive | Strangers | 3 |
| *An Isolated Incident* — JD Kirk | Reviewed series-positive | DCI Logan | 11 |
| *The Corfe Castle Murders* — Rachel McLean | Reviewed series-positive | Dorset Crime | 1 |
| *A Thousand Li: The Second Sect* — Tao Wong | Reviewed series-positive | A Thousand Li | 5 |

The selection frame is [ChannelX's complete shortlist report](https://channelx.world/2021/09/2021-kindle-storyteller-award-shortlist/).
Amazon's [winner announcement](https://www.aboutamazon.co.uk/news/small-businesses/the-corfe-castle-murders-by-rachel-mclean-crowned-winner-of-the-2021-kindle-storyteller-award)
independently confirms the KDP eligibility window and identifies *The Corfe Castle Murders* as the
winner. Neither source establishes gold series truth.

## Authority review

- Daisy James's [book catalog](https://daisyjames.co.uk/my-books) includes *Escape to the
  Hummingbird Hotel* under a dedicated Hummingbird Hotel Series heading. It does not explicitly
  number the work, so position remains unknown.
- Mary Kingswood's [Strangers series page](https://marykingswood.co.uk/index.php/series-strangers/)
  explicitly labels *Stranger at the Villa* book 3.
- JD Kirk's [exact title page](https://jdkirk.com/project/an-isolated-incident/) labels *An
  Isolated Incident* DCI Logan book 11; the author's [reading order](https://jdkirk.com/readingorder/)
  independently places it in that sequence.
- Rachel McLean's [exact-title post](https://www.rachelmclean.com/post/the-corfe-castle-murders-by-rachel-mclean-dorset-crime-book-1)
  labels *The Corfe Castle Murders* Dorset Crime book 1, while the author's
  [book catalog](https://www.rachelmclean.com/books) independently groups it in that series.
- Starlit Publishing's [exact product page](https://starlitpublishing.com/products/the-second-sect)
  labels *The Second Sect* A Thousand Li number 5 and calls it the fifth novel. The publisher's
  [series catalog](https://starlitpublishing.com/collections/a-thousand-li) independently includes
  the work.

## Program impact

After this frame, the development set contains 118 selected works: 108 reviewed and 10 candidates.
It contains 87 reviewed positives and 21 true standalone controls. The batch reduces the review
gap to 92, the positive gap to 13, and the recent independent or Kindle-first stratum gap to 23.

## Boundary

This batch changes only the reproducible trial data, audit test, and documentation. It does not
write Supabase, alter the corpus, deploy a function, or turn model output into gold truth.
