# Fern Michaels 2024–2025 matching-year development frame

Date reviewed: 2026-09-06

This frame adds every distinct title on Fern Michaels' complete annual release pages whose entry
in her complete official bibliography records the same year. The rule is fixed before provider
inspection and excludes reissues whose bibliography year predates the annual page. It yields five
2024 works and five 2025 works.

Selection sources:

- [2024 Releases](https://www.fernmichaels.com/2024-releases/)
- [2025 Releases](https://www.fernmichaels.com/2025-releases/)
- [Complete Book List](https://www.fernmichaels.com/complete-book-list/)

The annual pages establish the bounded selection frames, dates, and Kensington publication path.
The complete bibliography and exact publisher relationships establish gold truth. Selection-page
language is not used by itself as classification evidence.

## Gate impact

Before this frame, the development sample had 138 selected and 120 reviewed works, including 95
positive series cases and 25 true standalone controls. After review it has 148 selected and 127
reviewed works: 101 positive series cases, 26 true standalone controls, 34 reviewed recent
traditional works, and 18 reviewed multi-series or connected-universe works.

This closes the 100-case positive-series minimum. The remaining development pressure is negative:
24 more reviewed true standalones are required, along with 73 total reviews.
