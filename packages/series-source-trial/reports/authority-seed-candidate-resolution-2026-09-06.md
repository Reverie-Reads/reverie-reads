# Authority seed-candidate resolution

Date: 2026-09-06

## Outcome

One of the final two Reverie-seed candidates is now authority reviewed. *The Sacrifice* has direct
first-party membership evidence. *Bulletproof* remains intentionally unresolved rather than
preserving a likely false-positive seed tuple.

## The Sacrifice

Shantel Tessier's [exact title page](https://shanteltessier.com/the-sacrifice/) establishes the
work. Her [trigger-warning catalog](https://shanteltessier.com/trigger-warnings/) then places the
exact title under the “A Dark College Romance” heading. Gold truth therefore records that series
membership.

The seed calls the relationship “LORDS” and assigns position 3. Neither claim is explicit in the
cited first-party text, so gold truth uses the author's displayed grouping name and leaves position
unknown. This is a correction case, not a mechanical confirmation of the seed tuple.

## Bulletproof

Sourcebooks' [exact product page](https://www.sourcebooks.com/9781464265587-bulletproof-standard-edition-tp.html)
describes *Bulletproof* as set in the world of the Dark Forces, but it does not assign a series or
series number in the product attributes. By contrast, the publisher assigns an explicit Dark
Forces series and number to [*Your Knife, My Heart*](https://www.sourcebooks.com/9781464260131-your-knife-my-heart-deluxe-edition-tp.html)
and [*My Blade, Your Back*](https://www.sourcebooks.com/9781464260179-my-blade-your-back-standard-edition-tp.html).

That asymmetric publisher record makes the seed's “Dark Forces, position 4” claim unsafe. However,
missing series metadata is not affirmative standalone evidence, and world language cannot establish
a bibliographic relationship. The case therefore remains a candidate and contributes to neither
accuracy gate.

## Program impact

The development set remains 118 selected works and now contains 109 reviewed cases, 88 positive
series cases, 21 true standalone controls, and 9 candidates. Reverie's seeded-series stratum is
68 of 69 reviewed.

## Boundary

This review changes trial truth only. It does not alter the seed, corpus, Supabase, or production
classification.
